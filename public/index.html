Background

I did an undergraduate masters in Informatics (MInf) at the University of Edinburgh, worked as a programmer for a bit, then changed my mind and went back to University. I'm currently pursuing a PhD in artificial intelligence at the Institute for Language, Cognition and Computation at the University of Edinburgh.

Research

I'm interested in how machines can deal with the unexpected. Given huge amounts of data and enough time, computers can learn the best way to perform specific tasks. However, what happens when you slightly change the task, teach the machine a new skill, or reveal that the initial problem was more complicated than it first appeared?
